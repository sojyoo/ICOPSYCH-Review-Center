## Chapter 4 Overview

This chapter presents comprehensive results and discussion of the adaptive review planning system for Psychometrician Licensure Examination preparation. The study successfully:

1. **Analyzed comprehensive datasets** from multiple sources (154 training students, 44 survey respondents, 564 recommendations)

2. **Compared five machine learning models** with fair training procedures:
   - **Top-tier**: Random Forest (93.55% accuracy) - **Best Performer**
   - **Second-tier**: Curriculum Learning (87.10% accuracy)
   - **Third-tier**: Bayesian Optimization (83.87% accuracy), Multi-Armed Bandits (83.87% accuracy)
   - **Fourth-tier**: Deep Learning (70.97% accuracy)

3. **Implemented production-ready system** with three-tier architecture, robust fallback mechanisms, and real-time recommendation generation

4. **Systematically answered all research questions** from the Statement of the Problem:
   - Identified most effective features (performance-based metrics)
   - Evaluated machine learning techniques (Random Forest optimal)
   - Established evaluation metrics (all four essential)
   - Determined best model (Random Forest with 93.55% accuracy)

5. **Provided comprehensive discussion** of results, limitations, future work, and practical implications

The chapter demonstrates that machine learning can effectively support personalized study planning through risk classification and recommendation generation, with Random Forest emerging as the optimal choice for practical deployment due to its combination of superior accuracy, interpretability, and efficiency.
